# QueryLabs Studio

Right now there's nothing you can use, but in the future (I hope)
there will be a lightweight desktop app for easy database management :)

**Everything below is the roadmap from my perspective.**

## Functionality

### Quick access to your databases
You can connect to your PostgreSQL, write SQL code, run queries,
manage tables and hold as many connections as you need — absolutely free.

### Personalize your app
Choose theme and language pack or make up your own appearance.

### Native and fast
- Backend built with Go
- Services and components communicate over gRPC

### What this app won't have
- No Internet required for self-hosted database connections
- No accounts
- No subscriptions
- No connection limits

### About
QueryLabs Studio is my experiment to build a fast app for comfortable database
management and replace other bloated apps with restrictive limits.
